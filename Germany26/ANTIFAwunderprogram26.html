Constituency data
Seed convention:
The opening value of your country's main stock index on the first trading day of the week before election day (normally Monday; if that is a holiday, the next trading day).
The value is rounded to a whole number — 45123.78, 45 123,78 and 45124 all count as 45124, so small differences between published sources do not matter.
Source: newspaper financial section · TV/radio market report · bank app.
Candidates and weight
Enter each candidate's estimated popularity in % — a previous election result or any honest estimate. The tool snaps it to a coarse logarithmic weight bucket (1 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 8 · 12 · 18 · 27 · 40 · 60), shown live next to the field. The bucket is the number of "lottery tickets" in the hash draw. Nearby estimates land in the same bucket, so small disagreements between people do not change the result.
Ballot # is optional: only needed if the tool reports that two names are identical after normalization. Use the candidates' official position numbers on the ballot paper.
